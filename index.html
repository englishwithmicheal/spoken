Every listening task and every Say It Aloud box in the book has a recording. Scan the code printed beside the task to open it here, or find the track number below. Play each recording as often as you need, and pause it to repeat or to write.

About these recordings. The voices are computer-generated British English voices, made for this book. The tunes of the voice in the intonation tasks (falls and rises) were set by hand to match what the book teaches, and every recording was checked word by word with speech-recognition software. A teacher's own voice is still the best model in class: use the recordings for practice, for revision and for listening at home.
